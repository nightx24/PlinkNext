# PocketLink Quick Start Guide

Welcome to PocketLink — your private personal storage server & media streamer.

## What's Included for Testing
- **Music**: 3 harmonic synth & lo-fi MP3 tracks (`Ambient_Dawn_Chords.mp3`, `Midnight_Pulse_Synth.mp3`, `LoFi_Horizon_Keys.mp3`).
- **Videos**: 2 H.264/AAC MP4 video clips (`PocketLink_Orbit_Demo.mp4`, `Neon_Fractal_Visualizer.mp4`).
- **Pictures**: 3 high-resolution photos (`Alpine_Twilight.jpg`, `Tokyo_Rain_Neon.jpg`, `Studio_SSD_Desk.jpg`).
- **Documents**: Quick start guide & storage checklist.

## Gestures on the Floating Dock
1. **Tap** Home, Explore, or Files (or swipe left/right between tabs).
2. **Tap** the Music / Play pill to open Now Playing or start playback.
3. **Swipe left/right** on the Music / Play pill (or Mini Player) to change tracks.
4. **Hold (long-press)** the Music / Play pill to expand quick shortcuts (Files, Photos, Documents, Music, Videos, Upload).
